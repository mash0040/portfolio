# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring readers across three tiers, all visiting the same surfaces:

- **Non-technical recruiters** skimming for title, stack, availability, contact. They make a 30-second decision.
- **Engineering hiring managers** reading case studies to judge problem framing and decision-making.
- **Senior engineers** reviewing project specifics, architecture choices, and code-quality signals. The toughest reader.

Single visit, evaluative mindset. They came to decide whether to talk to Ekene, not to use a product.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Ekene Masha that drives contact and interview conversion across role levels (junior to mid full-stack), engagement types (full-time, freelance), and reader profiles (recruiter to senior engineer). Built to be a stable long-term credibility anchor, not just a job-search artifact.

Success: the toughest reader (a senior engineer skimming for depth signals) leaves with enough to send a Slack message saying "talk to this one."

## Brand Personality

**Ships complete things. The site itself is the proof.**

Three-word framing: practical, plain-spoken, complete.

Voice: confident without bravado. Specific over adjective-heavy. Closer to a well-edited engineering README than a marketing landing. The site demonstrates the engineering claims it makes; aesthetic discipline is itself a depth signal.

## Anti-references

Things this site must not look or feel like:

- **brittanychiang.com clone aesthetic.** Dark background plus purple accent, sticky sidebar nav, scroll-spy section indicator. The single most-cloned dev portfolio template; instant signal of "I copied a tutorial."
- **AI SaaS landing template.** Hero with stacked CTA pair, icon grid for skills, purple gradients, identical card grid for projects. The training-data reflex.
- **Awwwards-bait.** Scroll-triggered animation as the product, custom cursors, motion design carrying the show. Wrong signal for an engineering role.
- **itssharl.ee-level animation.** Beautiful for a designer's portfolio, a credibility tax on an engineer's. Atmosphere and calm from that site translate; the motion design does not.

Reference points (atmosphere, not imitation): itssharl.ee for calm and restraint, leerob.com for content-first density without filler.

## Design Principles

1. **Site as proof.** The portfolio is itself an artifact of the work it claims. Every craft choice (typography, spacing, performance, accessibility) is a depth signal that survives close reading.
2. **Magazine front, utility back.** Hero and About lean editorial: pacing, typography, whitespace carry identity. Projects index and Contact lean utility: high density, fast extraction, low-friction handoff. Same voice across both modes.
3. **Specific over decorative.** Concrete claims with verbs and nouns beat adjectives. Calm restraint reads as confidence; flourish reads as compensation.
4. **All audiences, one read.** The 30-second recruiter and the 5-minute senior engineer share one site. Hierarchy is the path between them: skim layer surfaces facts, depth layer rewards the reader who stays.
5. **Anti-template.** Refuse the portfolio archetypes (purple-accent dark mode, icon-grid skills, identical project cards). When a layout reads like a template, replace its structure, not its color.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Full keyboard navigation. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No color-only signals (state communicated via text, weight, or icon in addition to color). Sufficient contrast on slate body text and accent links.
